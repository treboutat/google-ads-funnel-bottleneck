# Sources & basis

This skill encodes TNT Growth's funnel bottleneck diagnostic — the stage-by-stage framework used before touching any ad account.

## The 5-stage model and decision tree
TNT's standard diagnostic sequence for paid acquisition: localize the broken stage first, then route to the specific fix. The core discipline — one bottleneck, upstream tie-break, never scale spend onto a broken downstream stage — is operating doctrine, not theory.

## The benchmarks
The healthy ranges in `references/funnel-benchmarks.md` (LP CVR 3-8% cold paid B2B, show rates, close rates) are operating heuristics from TNT-managed accounts and general industry experience. They are explicitly guidance, not verdicts — the skill always prefers the user's own historical baseline and says when it's falling back to a table.

## Volume floors
The sub-$10K/mo "noise dominates" warning and the $25K/mo reliability threshold reflect practical experience with small-sample stage math (a close-rate "collapse" on a handful of deals is usually noise). Heuristics, not statistics theorems.

## What this skill deliberately does not claim
- No specific client names or client results.
- No guaranteed outcomes — expected-impact math is always computed from the user's own numbers.
- No universal benchmarks — every table is labeled as fallback guidance.
